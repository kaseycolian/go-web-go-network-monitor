Latency by layer

gap between router and internet lines = where the problem lives
Each line is a ping target checked every few seconds, one per network layer:
router — your own router, the first hop; trouble here means the problem is inside your home (cable, dock, WiFi, router).
1.1.1.1 / 8.8.8.8 — Cloudflare's and Google's public servers, two independent anchors out on the internet.
ISP hop — the first router past yours, inside your ISP's network (found automatically with a traceroute).
Reading it: lines bunched together = healthy. Internet lines high or gapped while the router line stays low = the problem is beyond your home, at the ISP.
